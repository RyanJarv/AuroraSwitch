AuroraSwitch installer

Development build — physical installation and recovery are not qualified.

    Dirt Verb is required. Other supported images are optional.

    USB installation: place AuroraSwitch.bin at the drive root, safely eject, insert into Aurora and power on. Keep one root BIN.

    Shift — category. Reverse — firmware. Freeze — verify & launch. Shift + Reverse at power-on — menu. FataMorgana wavetables still require USB.
